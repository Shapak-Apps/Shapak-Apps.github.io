Privacy Policy — Hytaý dili 1

Effective date: 13 August 2026 · Developer: Seydi Charyyev (Şapak Apps)

Summary

Hytaý dili 1 does not collect any data. The app works fully offline, has no accounts, no registration, no analytics and no advertising.

Data collection

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. Your learning progress, XP, streak, bookmarks and the name you enter during onboarding are stored only on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Backups

The optional backup feature creates a file on your device that you can export and import yourself. It is never uploaded anywhere by the app.
